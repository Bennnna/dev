About

I'm Jiwoo Jung

M.S., Department of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Dongguk University, Seoul.

AI Researcher specializing in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, reasoning–physics consistency auditing, multimodal AI, and autonomous driving. Published at CVPR 2026 and in the SCIE Q1 journal Complex & Intelligent Systems.

Physical-Margin Chain-of-Contradiction (PhysMargin-CoC) — Developed a deterministic post-hoc auditing framework that identifies inconsistencies between action-oriented VLA reasoning and independently computed physical, comfort, and behavioral evidence. The framework evaluates Waymo Open Motion Dataset scenarios using six signed margins, text-independent applicability masks, and an inspectable 12 × 6 predicate–margin contradiction matrix. It also provides pairwise evidence attribution and paired Base/DoRA model evaluation.

인공지능융합혁신인재양성(IITP/MSIT) — Designed a Vision-Language-Action pipeline incorporating inference-time reasoning methods, including Visual Chain-of-Thought and Tree-of-Thought evaluation, for autonomous-driving decision analysis. This work led to publication at CVPR 2026.

ICAN AI+X Project (IITP/MSIT) — Developed a Physics-Informed Neural Network combined with momentum contrastive learning for battery state-of-health estimation and filed a patent on AI-driven physical-system monitoring. Published in Complex & Intelligent Systems.

AI 운전능력평가 표준화사업(KIPoT/경찰청) — Designed AI-based evaluation metrics and benchmark scenarios for autonomous-driving capability assessment and developed a standardized testing pipeline integrating vision-language models for driving-behavior analysis.
